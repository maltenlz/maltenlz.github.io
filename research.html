Research

During my PhD and postdoc at LMU Munich I worked at the intersection of classical statistics, machine learning and evaluation: how to compare models fairly, how to quantify their uncertainty, and how to keep them interpretable. Peer-reviewed publications and dissertation below; see also my Google Scholar profile.
